04 / GOOD TO KNOW

A few things
worth knowing.

Can Finora connect to my bank account?+

No. Finora is built for manually tracking and organizing your finances; it doesn't sync bank accounts or move money.

What can I track in Finora?+

Income, expenses, categories, budgets, savings goals, investments, and financial summaries.

Can I use it on my phone?+

Finora is a responsive web application designed for modern desktop and mobile browsers.

Is this financial or investment advice?+

No. Finora helps you organize and review your own information; its charts and summaries aren't professional financial advice.
